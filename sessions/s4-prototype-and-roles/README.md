# Session 4 - From document to clickable prototype, and roles

**Theme of the live session:** a requirements document is hard to judge until you can click it. In this session you turn a BRD into a working prototype, then meet the idea of roles: a subagent is a colleague with one job and limited access. It works in its own room and returns a short report. A skill, by comparison, is a recipe: it tells you how to do something. A subagent is a colleague: it does the thing, separately from you.
