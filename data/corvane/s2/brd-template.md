# BRD template (Corvane Supply)

A BRD (business requirements document) says what the business needs, in plain words, before anyone builds anything. Keep it short. One page per section is plenty.

Rules for filling it in:
- Use only what was said or written in the sources. If it was not said, do not write it as a fact. Put it under Open questions.
- Every requirement cites its source: file name, date, and minute, for example (kickoff 2026-10-14, 02:31).
- If a decision changed between two sources, write the latest version and add one line: "Changed from ..., see ...".

## 1. Background
Why this project exists. Two to four sentences with the numbers people actually said.

## 2. Goal and success metric
One goal sentence. Then the success metric: what will be measurably different, with a number if one was given. If no number was given, say so and move it to Open questions.

## 3. Scope
In scope:
- ...

Out of scope:
- ...

## 4. Users
Who will use it and what each group does with it. One line per group.

## 5. Business requirements

### BR-1 Short title
Requirement: one sentence starting with "The business needs ...".
Acceptance criteria:
- A test someone can check, written as "Given ... when ... then ...", or a plain checkable statement.
Source: file, date, minute.

(Repeat for BR-2, BR-3, and so on.)

## 6. Business rules
Numbered rules (rules, thresholds, formulas), each with its source.

## 7. Open questions
Anything not decided or not said. Each with the person who could answer it, if known.

## 8. Sources
The list of files used, with dates.
