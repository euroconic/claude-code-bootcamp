# Policy 05: Approval Matrix

Owner: Operational Excellence (Paweł Nowicki). Version 4.0, effective 2026-04-01.

## 1. How to read this matrix
Amounts are in PLN and are the total of the document, not of one line. Every limit is inclusive of the upper value: for example 1,000.00 PLN falls in the first band. For a EUR document, convert at the NBP rate of the invoice date.

## 2. Credit notes (Policy 04)

| Credit note total | Approver |
|---|---|
| up to 1,000.00 PLN | Reconciliation specialist |
| 1,000.01 to 5,000.00 PLN | Head of Supplier Reconciliation |
| 5,000.01 to 50,000.00 PLN | COO |
| above 50,000.00 PLN | COO and CFO together |

So a credit note above 5,000 PLN is approved by the COO. Only when it is above 50,000.00 PLN does the CFO also sign.

## 3. Purchase order changes (Policy 08)

| Change in PO value | Approver |
|---|---|
| up to 2,000.00 PLN | Buyer |
| 2,000.01 to 20,000.00 PLN | Head of Procurement |
| above 20,000.00 PLN | COO |

## 4. New suppliers (Policy 01)
Head of Procurement. Expected annual order value above 500,000.00 PLN: also the COO.

## 5. Absence
If the approver is absent for more than 3 working days, their deputy approves. The deputy is named in the ERP. A person never approves a document that they requested.
