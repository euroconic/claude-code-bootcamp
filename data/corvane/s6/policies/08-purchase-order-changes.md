# Policy 08: Purchase Order Changes

Owner: Procurement. Version 1.9, effective 2026-03-15.

## 1. Rule
A purchase order (PO) is changed only in the ERP, never only by email. The supplier receives the changed PO and confirms it.

## 2. What counts as a change
1. A different quantity on any line.
2. A different agreed unit price.
3. A new or removed line.
4. A different delivery address.

## 3. Approval
The approver depends on the change in total PO value, as in Policy 05 (Approval Matrix), section 3. A change that lowers the value needs the same approval as one that raises it.

## 4. Price changes after invoicing
1. If the supplier invoices a price different from the PO, the PO is not changed to match the invoice.
2. The difference is handled as a discrepancy (Policy 03).
3. Only if both sides agree the new price in writing, the buyer changes the PO and records the reason.

## 5. Version history
The ERP keeps every version of a PO. Reconciliation always checks an invoice against the PO version valid on the invoice date.

## 6. Closing a PO
A PO is closed when all lines are received and invoiced, or by the buyer with a written reason.
