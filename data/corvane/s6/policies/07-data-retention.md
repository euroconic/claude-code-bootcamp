# Policy 07: Data Retention

Owner: Operational Excellence (Paweł Nowicki), with Legal. Version 2.1, effective 2026-01-01.

## 1. Retention periods

| Document | Keep for |
|---|---|
| Supplier invoices, credit notes | 5 years after the end of the financial year |
| Purchase orders and PO change records | 5 years after the end of the financial year |
| Discrepancy logs and comment trails | 5 years after the end of the financial year |
| Supplier onboarding files | 5 years after the supplier is set to inactive |
| Meeting notes and follow-ups | 2 years |
| Draft documents and working copies | 6 months after the final version |

## 2. Where documents are kept
The official copy lives in the ERP or in the document archive. Working copies on personal drives are not an archive.

## 3. Personal data
1. Personal data of supplier contacts (name, email, phone) is kept only as long as the supplier is active, plus 12 months.
2. Bank account numbers of private persons are never stored in working files.

## 4. Use of AI tools
1. Documents that contain personal data or bank account numbers are not given to an external AI tool unless Legal has approved that tool for that data.
2. Data given to an approved tool stays within the retention periods above.

## 5. Deletion
At the end of a retention period the owner of the document deletes it and records the deletion. Legal can place a hold that stops deletion.
