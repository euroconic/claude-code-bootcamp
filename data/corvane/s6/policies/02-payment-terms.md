# Policy 02: Payment Terms

Owner: Finance (Accounts Payable). Version 2.4, effective 2026-02-01.

## 1. Standard terms
1. Standard payment term is 30 days from the invoice date.
2. Suppliers of packaging and paper may be agreed at 45 days in the supplier contract.
3. The agreed term is stored in the ERP supplier record. The invoice text does not change it.

## 2. Payment runs
1. Corvane Supply pays suppliers twice a month, on the 10th and the 25th.
2. An invoice due between two runs is paid in the earlier run.
3. If the 10th or the 25th is not a working day, the run moves to the previous working day.

## 3. Currency
1. Invoices in EUR are paid in EUR.
2. For reporting, EUR amounts are converted to PLN at the National Bank of Poland (NBP) table A mid rate of the invoice date. If there is no rate for that date, use the rate of the previous business day.

## 4. Payment holds
1. An invoice with an open discrepancy above tolerance (Policy 03) is not paid in full until the discrepancy is resolved.
2. Lines without discrepancies from the same invoice may be paid on time.
3. A hold longer than 14 days is reported to the Head of Supplier Reconciliation.

## 5. Early payment discounts
A discount offered for early payment is taken only if Finance confirms cash availability. Reconciliation does not decide this.
