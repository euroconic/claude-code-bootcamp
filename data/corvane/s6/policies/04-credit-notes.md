# Policy 04: Credit Notes

Owner: Supplier Reconciliation (Marta Kowal). Version 1.6, effective 2026-03-01.

## 1. When a credit note is requested
1. When a discrepancy above tolerance (Policy 03) is confirmed as the supplier's error.
2. When goods are returned and accepted by the supplier (Policy 06).
3. When a price agreed in the PO was not applied on the invoice.

## 2. Who requests
The buyer who owns the PO requests the credit note in writing. Reconciliation attaches the discrepancy log entry.

## 3. What a credit note must show
1. Reference to the original invoice number.
2. Reference to the PO number.
3. Amount, in the currency of the original invoice.
4. The reason, in one line.

## 4. Approval
1. A credit note is approved before it is booked. The approver depends on the amount, as set in Policy 05 (Approval Matrix).
2. The amount is the credit note total in PLN. For a EUR credit note, convert at the NBP rate of the date of the original invoice.
3. A credit note cannot be split into several smaller notes to stay under an approval limit.

## 5. Timing
1. A credit note should be received within 10 working days of the request.
2. If it is not received, the buyer escalates to the Head of Supplier Reconciliation.
3. Until the credit note arrives, the disputed amount stays on hold (Policy 02, section 4).

## 6. Alternative
The supplier may issue a corrected invoice instead. In that case the original invoice is cancelled in the ERP and no credit note is booked.
