# Policy 06: Returns

Owner: Warehouse Operations. Version 2.2, effective 2026-02-15.

## 1. Reasons for a return
1. Goods damaged on arrival.
2. Wrong item or wrong quantity delivered against the PO.
3. Goods that do not meet the specification in the PO.

## 2. Deadline
Goods must be reported to the supplier within 5 working days of receipt. After that, the return is accepted only if the supplier agrees.

## 3. Procedure
1. The warehouse photographs the goods and the delivery note and records the case in the ERP.
2. The buyer contacts the supplier and asks for a return authorization number.
3. Goods are stored apart from normal stock, marked "return", until collected.
4. The warehouse records the date and the person who handed the goods to the supplier.

## 4. Financial result
1. Returned goods lead to a credit note (Policy 04, section 1).
2. If the supplier agrees to replace the goods instead, no credit note is requested and the replacement is recorded against the same PO.
3. Return transport is paid by the supplier when the error is the supplier's.

## 5. Customer returns
Returns from Corvane customers follow the customer terms and are not covered by this policy.
