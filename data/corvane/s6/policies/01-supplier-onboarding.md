# Policy 01: Supplier Onboarding

Owner: Operational Excellence (Paweł Nowicki). Version 3.1, effective 2026-01-15.

## 1. Purpose
Every new supplier is checked and registered before Corvane Supply places a first purchase order (PO).

## 2. Documents required
1. Company registration extract, not older than 3 months.
2. Tax ID (NIP for Polish suppliers, VAT-EU number for suppliers in other EU countries).
3. Bank account confirmation on company letterhead, or a bank statement header.
4. Signed supplier code of conduct.

## 3. Checks
1. Procurement checks the tax ID against the official VAT register.
2. Reconciliation confirms the bank account matches the company name.
3. A supplier with a bank account in a country different from its registered country needs a written explanation.

## 4. Approval
A new supplier is activated in the ERP only after sign-off by the Head of Procurement. Annual order value expected above 500,000.00 PLN also needs the COO's sign-off (see Policy 05, Approval Matrix).

## 5. Changes to supplier data
A change of bank account is never accepted by email alone. Reconciliation calls the supplier on the phone number already in the ERP and records the call in the supplier file.

## 6. Review
Active suppliers are reviewed once a year. A supplier with no orders for 24 months is set to inactive.
