# Policy 03: Discrepancy Tolerance

Owner: Supplier Reconciliation (Marta Kowal). Version 2.0, effective 2026-10-22. Replaces version 1.x, which used a flat 50 PLN limit.

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines when a difference between a supplier invoice line and the purchase order (PO) line is accepted without further action.

## 2. The rule
1. The tolerance is 1% of the line value on the PO.
2. The minimum tolerance is 20.00 PLN per line. The tolerance for a line is whichever is higher: 1% of the line value, or 20.00 PLN.
3. The tolerance applies to each line separately. Differences on different lines are never added together.
4. A line with a quantity difference is never within tolerance by value alone. It is always reviewed (see section 3).

## 3. Quantity differences
Any difference in delivered or invoiced quantity against the PO is a discrepancy, whatever its value.

## 4. Lines in EUR
For a line in EUR, convert the difference to PLN at the NBP rate of the invoice date (Policy 02, section 3) and apply the same tolerance in PLN.

## 5. What happens
1. Difference within tolerance: the line is accepted and marked "within tolerance" in the discrepancy log. No credit note is needed.
2. Difference above tolerance: the line is marked "discrepancy". The buyer contacts the supplier. A credit note or a corrected invoice is requested (Policy 04).
3. The decision "accepted within tolerance" is made by a reconciliation specialist and does not need approval.

## 6. Example
PO line value 2,350.00 PLN. 1% is 23.50 PLN, which is higher than the 20.00 PLN minimum, so the tolerance is 23.50 PLN. A difference of 23.00 PLN is accepted. A difference of 24.00 PLN is a discrepancy.
