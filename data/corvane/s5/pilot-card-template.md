# 30-Day Pilot Card

Copy this file to `workspace/work/my-30-day-pilot.md` and fill every line. Use your own job, not Corvane. Numbers beat adjectives: "6 hours a month" is better than "a lot of time".

## 1. The chore
One sentence: what is the repeated task? (Example: "Every Monday I turn the weekly sales export into a status email for my director.")

## 2. Who does it now
Name or role, and how many people do the same chore.

## 3. Hours per month today
Hours each time x times per month = hours per month. Show the calculation.

## 4. Input files
What file types go in? (Example: Excel export, PDF invoices, meeting transcript, email thread.) Where do they live today? Add one real example file name.

## 5. Output
What does the finished result look like, and who reads it? (Example: a one-page Word document sent to the finance team.)

## 6. Success metric
How will you know after 30 days that it worked? Give a number. (Example: "Time per report drops from 90 minutes to 30 minutes, and my manager finds no wrong numbers in 4 reports out of 4.")

## 7. Data safety check
What data in these files must NOT go to the agent (personal data, bank details, contracts, anything your company forbids)? How will you remove or hide it? Who in your company would you ask if unsure?

## 8. First 30-minute step
One small thing you can do in half an hour. Write the exact action and the date. (Example: "On Thursday 20 November I take one anonymized weekly export and ask the agent to draft the email.")

## 9. Check-in dates
Three dates: day 7, day 14, day 30. At each one, answer: what worked, what broke, what do I change?
