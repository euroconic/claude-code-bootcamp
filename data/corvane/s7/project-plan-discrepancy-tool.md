# Project plan: Supplier discrepancy tool, version 1

Project owner: Tomasz Wren (Project Office). Business owner: Marta Kowal (Head of Supplier Reconciliation). Sponsor: Lena Brandt (COO).
Plan version: 3, dated 2026-10-16. Tracker key: COR-101. Release date: 2026-11-15.

## Goal

Catch price and quantity mismatches between supplier invoices and purchase orders before payment, show them on one screen, and let the reconciliation team resolve each one with a comment trail.

## Scope of version 1

In: price mismatch, quantity mismatch, one screen with a list, a comment per line, a daily refresh from the ERP export.
Out: automatic corrections, supplier-facing messages, any change to payment runs.
